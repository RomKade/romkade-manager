ROMKADE Manager

Your retro handheld, managed from your computer.

Starting…

  1. Create your ROMKADE account in the Manager.
  2. Turn the console on, on the same Wi-Fi as this computer: the Manager finds it and guides you through connecting it.
  3. To talk to it, add the ROMKADE connector in ChatGPT or Claude.

Installation guide

Uninstalling this extension deletes ROMKADE Manager's data on this computer (library, settings, copies of saves). Your games and saves on the console are not affected.
